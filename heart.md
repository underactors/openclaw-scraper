# Who is Davon Cudjoe

Davon is a 36-year-old entrepreneur and father of three based in Couva, Trinidad and Tobago. He is the founder and CEO of RUGGTECH, Caribbean Flavor Pimentos, and an active forex/algorithmic trading developer. Before entrepreneurship, he served in tactical policing.

## Why RUGGTECH exists

Davon is building generational wealth for his children — D'Angelo, Danessa, and their sibling. RUGGTECH is not a side project. It is the vehicle through which he intends to create a legacy business that his children can inherit and grow. Every dollar of ad spend, every customer conversation, every new region opened — it all connects back to this purpose.

He homeschools his children while running three businesses simultaneously. D'Angelo (his son) works alongside him learning product photography, write-ups, and business operations. This is a family operation being professionalized through technology and AI.

## What drives him

- Self-reliance — He left a stable policing career to bet on himself. He is not backed by investors or a corporate structure. Every TTD of ad spend is real money from his pocket.
- Proving the Caribbean can compete globally — He is building an international distribution company from Trinidad, not Silicon Valley. This matters to him deeply. RUGGTECH succeeding as a Caribbean company is part of the mission.
- Efficiency and systems thinking — He practices Dr. Joe Dispenza walking meditation and Dr. Lloyd Glauberman's Hypno-Peripheral Processing. He thinks in systems. He wants processes that run without him so he can focus on strategy and his children.
- Honesty and directness — He does not want sugar-coating. He wants real numbers, real problems, real recommendations. If something is failing, tell him immediately so he can redirect.
- Learning as building — He built the entire RUGGTECH tech stack himself: Next.js storefront, Sanity CMS, Clerk auth, PayPal + USDT payments, Shippo fulfillment, React Native mobile app. He understands what he is building and demands that you do too.

## How he makes decisions

Davon is a "show me the plan before you build" thinker. He does not want surprises. He wants:
1. The problem or opportunity clearly stated
2. The recommended action with reasoning
3. The cost (in TTD and time)
4. The expected outcome with realistic numbers
5. His approval before execution

He will say yes to most well-reasoned proposals. He will say no to anything vague or wasteful. He respects competence and punishes laziness.
